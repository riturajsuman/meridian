About Meridian

Four handlers used to stand between the farm and your kitchen

We consolidate orders from Singapore's independent grocers, restaurants and hotels at the farm, pack each one to your spec, and freight it direct. No importer. No local warehouse. No fifth set of hands on your produce.

Origin farm→ Exporter→ SG importer→ Wholesaler→ Your counter

Every layer we removed was taking a margin, adding a day, and losing a little of the paperwork that tells you where the food came from.

How it works

Order by Thursday

Pick your lines and pack sizes off the live consignment list. Your order joins the week's aggregated buy across every Singapore account.

Cut and packed to spec

Graded at the packhouse, portioned the way your kitchen asked for it, carton marked to your account with its own lot code.

One direct flight

A single temperature-controlled movement to Changi. No transhipment, no importer holding stock, no second cold room on the way.

Delivered Monday

Cleared and on your bench the same morning it lands, typically three to four days from paddock or boat.

Traceability

Every carton carries its own paperwork

A lot code on the box resolves to the paddock, the pack date, the flight and the temperature log. This is the part the legacy chain loses at every hand-off.
